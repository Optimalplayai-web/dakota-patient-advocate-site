Before you call

Questions people ask most

What happens on the free call?

You describe the situation in your own words. I ask a few clarifying questions, then tell you honestly whether patient advocacy would help, roughly what it would involve, and what it would cost. If it isn't a fit, I'll say so and point you somewhere more useful. There's no pressure and no follow-up sales sequence.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within a few days for planned work. Hospital and discharge situations are prioritized — if something is happening now, call (701) 319-1700 rather than using the form.

Can you help if my family member lives here and I don't?

Yes, and this is one of the most common reasons people call. I attend appointments and hospital visits in person and keep you fully informed wherever you are, with your family member's written authorization.

Do you work with people outside Bismarck–Mandan?

Yes. Records review, appeals, research, telehealth attendance, and coordination all work virtually anywhere in North Dakota. In-person travel elsewhere in the state is arranged case by case.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Everything you share is confidential and disclosed only to people and organizations you authorize in writing. Please keep detailed medical information out of the web form — we'll handle those details securely once we're in touch.
