Why this exists

You shouldn't have to fight the system while you're trying to get well.

Appointments run fifteen minutes. Specialists don't talk to each other. Insurance denies a medication with no explanation you can follow. A parent is being discharged tomorrow and no one has said where they're going.

After more than two decades in cardiology, internal medicine, family practice, and behavioral health, I watched capable, intelligent people leave appointments more confused than when they walked in — not because they weren't paying attention, but because the system isn't built to be understood.

That's the gap I fill. I know how hospitals, clinics, and insurers actually work from the inside, and I use that knowledge entirely on your behalf.

How I help

Practical support at the moments that matter most

Every engagement is built around what you actually need — a single appointment, one stubborn insurance denial, or steady support through a long illness.

Appointments & Care Coordination

I go with you to appointments, help you prepare the questions that matter, take notes, and make sure your concerns are actually heard — then keep every provider on the same page afterward.

Billing & Insurance Help

Medication prior authorizations, confusing statements, and denied claims. I review the paperwork, find the error or the argument, and write the appeal.

Transitions of Care

Searching for assisted living, memory care, or a nursing home, then coordinating the move so nothing — medications, records, or dignity — gets lost along the way.

New Diagnosis Support

A new diagnosis arrives with a wall of information and very little time. I translate it into plain language and help you think through the next step.

Hospital Advocacy & Discharge Planning

Someone at the bedside asking the right questions, and a discharge plan that's genuinely safe — not just a signature on a form.

Medical Records & Review

Gathering, organizing, and reviewing scattered records so you and your providers finally have the whole picture in one place.

Independent by design

No hospital, clinic, or insurer pays me. You do.

That single fact changes everything about the advice you get. Case managers and discharge planners are good people working inside institutions that also employ them. My only obligation is to you.

  • No referral fees, kickbacks, or facility commissions — ever
  • No pressure toward a particular provider, plan, or placement
  • Straight answers, including when the answer is “you don't need to hire me for this”
  • Your information stays confidential and shared only where you direct
